# Week 1 | The BPM Professional and BPM CBOK®

## 1.1: Guide to the Business Process Management Common Body of Knowledge (BPM CBOK®) Version 4.0
Professional Body of Knowledge Guide
Source: ABPMP International 2019, BPM CBOK® Version 4.0: Guide to the Business Process Management Common Body of Knowledge, Association of Business Process Management Professionals, ISBN: 9781704809342, viewed 8/08/2029, https://www.abpmp.org/page/guide_BPM_CBOK.

The BPM CBOK® Version 4.0 serves as the foundational reference for business process management professionals, published by the Association of Business Process Management Professionals (ABPMP). This comprehensive guide defines the scope of BPM as a disciplined management approach to identify, design, execute, document, measure, monitor, and control business processes. It outlines the knowledge areas that BPM professionals must master, including process identification, modelling, analysis, and governance. The guide establishes professional standards and competencies required for effective BPM practice, emphasising the integration of business and IT perspectives in process management initiatives.


## Business Process Management as a Career – Opportunities and Pathways
Industry Report
Source: CIO Insight 2025, 'The state of BPM careers: Opportunities and pathways in 2025', CIO Insight, 15 January, viewed 8/08/2029, https://www.cioinsight.com

This industry report examines the current state of BPM careers, exploring the diverse job opportunities available to professionals with BPM skills and competencies. The report identifies growing demand for roles including Business Process Analyst, Process Improvement Analyst, Digital Transformation Consultant, Business Architect, and Change Analyst. It analyses the skills employers are seeking, including technical proficiency in process modelling tools, analytical capabilities, change management expertise, and business acumen. The report also discusses career progression pathways and the importance of continuous professional development in the rapidly evolving BPM field.
