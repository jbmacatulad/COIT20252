## Week 4 | Business Process Modelling

#### **Artefact 1 – Quality and Accuracy in Modern BPMN Modelling**

**Artefact Type: Scholarly Journal Article**
**Source: Hörner, Möller and Reichert (2026)**




Hörner, Möller and Reichert (2026, p. 59) examine the automated creation of BPMN 2.0 models from written process descriptions using an AI-based framework called BPMNGen. Their study compares generated models with expert-created models and evaluates whether they accurately represent the intended process and remain understandable to users. The research shows that automated modelling performs well for simpler processes but becomes more challenging as complexity increases.

I chose this artefact because it changed how I think about a “good” process model. I initially focused mainly on whether BPMN symbols and sequence flows were technically correct. The article showed me that semantic accuracy and understandability are equally important. A process model must represent the actual business situation clearly enough for stakeholders to interpret and use it, not simply follow notation rules (Hörner, Möller & Reichert 2026, p. 60).

#### Artefact 2 – My BPMN Model: Enrol in a University Unit

**Artefact Type: Own Work – BPMN Swimlane Model
Tool: Bizagi Modeler, 2026**




I created a BPMN model in Bizagi for the process “Enrol in a University Unit.” I separated the process into Student, Student Portal/System and Course Management swimlanes. The model includes activities such as selecting a unit, checking prerequisites and confirming enrolment, together with gateways for prerequisite approval and class availability.

Creating the model helped me understand that process modelling involves more than arranging activities in sequence. Assigning activities to different lanes made responsibilities and handoffs much easier to identify, while gateways forced me to consider alternative outcomes rather than assuming that every enrolment follows one path. BPMN uses events, tasks and gateways to represent process behaviour and decision points clearly (Hörner, Möller & Reichert 2026, p. 61). This artefact demonstrates that I can apply modelling concepts to a process that is familiar to me.
