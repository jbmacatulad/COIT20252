## Week 6 | Process Redesign and Performance Measurement

#### **BPMN Supporting Lean Process Improvement**

**Artefact Type: Scholarly Case Study
Source: Costa and Ávila (2025)**


Costa and Ávila (2025, p. 6) demonstrate how BPMN was used in a wind-blade manufacturing process to model the current process, identify handoffs and redesign responsibilities. Following the improvement activities, measured cycle times decreased by approximately 27% at one production position, 7% at another and 20% at a third (Costa & Ávila 2025, p. 10).

I chose this artefact because it clearly connects process modelling with process redesign and performance measurement. My main learning was that a to-be model should not simply look simpler than the as-is model. Improvements need to address an identified problem and should produce measurable results. The case showed how modelling exposed responsibilities and inefficient handoffs before changes were implemented (Costa & Ávila 2025, p. 10). This demonstrates why modelling, redesign and measurement should operate together as part of continuous process improvement.
