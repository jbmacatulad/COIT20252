## Week 5 | Process Validation and Simulation

#### Artefact 3 – BPMN and Process Simulation

**Artefact Type: Scholarly Conference Article
Source: Cimino et al. (2025)**



Cimino et al. (2025, p. 3236) examine how simulation can extend BPMN beyond a static representation of a process. By combining BPMN with simulation, organisations can run different process scenarios, test changing conditions and investigate how resources or decisions may affect performance before implementing changes.

I selected this artefact because it corrected my earlier assumption that completing a BPMN diagram meant the modelling work was finished. I now understand that a model can also be used as an experimental tool. Simulation allows alternative process designs to be compared and potential problems to be investigated without disrupting the real process (Cimino et al. 2025, p. 3236). This is meaningful to my understanding of BPM because validation provides evidence that a proposed process is likely to work, rather than relying only on how logical the diagram appears.
